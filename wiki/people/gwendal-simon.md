**Organization**: Synamedia
**Role**: Co-driver of the SWITCH / track-switching design; [[moq-msfts|MSFTS]] author

# Contributions

- One of the two principals (with [[will-law|Will Law]]) behind the **SWITCH / DTS** track-switching design that became **[[switch-abr|SSTS]]** — *Sender-Side Track Switching* — after the June-22 interim renamed it.
- Raised the **per-switching-set DDoS-protection concerns** on the DTS [PR #1638](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1638) (concurrent-track and throughput limits). The June-22 interim resolved to **remove** those negotiation properties and rely on authorization tokens plus existing relay protections instead.
- Author of **[[moq-msfts]]** (`draft-gregoire-moq-msfts-00`) — MPEG-2 Transport Stream packaging for [[moq-msf|MSF]] (`m2ts`).
- Presented SWITCH issues at the London interim (June 12).
