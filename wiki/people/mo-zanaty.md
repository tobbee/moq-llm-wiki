**Organization**: Cisco
**GitHub**: [mzanaty](https://github.com/mzanaty)
**Role**: Filter design lead in [[moq-transport]]; [[moq-loc|LOC]] contributor

# Contributions

- **Range Filters** ([PR #1765](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1765)) — the one gated Design PR to make the **draft-19** cut (2026-07-06).
- **Location Filter redesign** to match the shape of the other filters ([PR #1809](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1809)) — merged 2026-08-13 in the first draft-20-bound batch.
- **Top Tracks Filter** ([PR #1830](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1830)) — OPEN; the filter that [[yu-you|Yu You]]'s Nokia relay work and [[moq-conditional-filtering]] both build on, and the subject of [[ian-swett|Ian Swett]]'s "Top Tracks and SSTS (or ABR in general)" list thread.
- Object Filters advocacy through the May–June 2026 consensus calls; LOC updates at the London interim.

# Context

Zanaty's filter work is the part of the transport draft with the most unresolved surface as of August 2026: the IETF-126 chairs' consensus call moves **range filters into a separate draft**, out of core transport (contested by [[cullen-jennings]]), while the Top-Tracks × SSTS interaction remains an open design question with no demonstrated implementation.
