**Organization**: Meta
**Role**: Media-over-QUIC implementer; author of the moq-encoder-player demo stack

# Contributions

- Built and maintains the **moq-encoder-player** browser demo stack, an early and widely-referenced end-to-end MoQ encode→publish→play demonstration. It **dropped MOQ-MI** in August 2026 in favour of the WG's converging media layers.
- Demoed against [[luke-curley|Luke Curley]]'s Hang stack during the **Vienna Hackathon** (July 2026).
- A recurring reference point in [[media-packaging]] and [[moq-media-interop]] discussions.
