# Moqtopus

- **Repo**: [kota-yata/Moqtopus](https://github.com/kota-yata/Moqtopus) — C++ on **MsQuic**, announced **2026-06-04**.
- Targets **draft-18**; registered in the [[interop-runner]] as the `moqtopus` **client** endpoint and running at-target on the current matrix.
- Purpose: bring MoQ ingest/playback into **Unreal Engine**, i.e. game-engine and interactive-3D consumption of MoQ media — a use case no other tracked implementation covers.

> **Naming hazard**: Yatagai also has an older, unrelated pub/sub experiment named [kota-yata/moqtail](https://github.com/kota-yata/moqtail) (TypeScript, draft-11, last pushed March 2026). It is **not** the tracked [[moqtail|moqtail/moqtail]] project by [[zafer-gurel|Zafer Gürel]] and [[ali-begen|Ali C. Begen]], despite the identical name.
