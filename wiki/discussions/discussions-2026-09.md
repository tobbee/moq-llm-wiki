**Bugs afrind and [[aman-sharma|Aman Sharma]] found in [[moqtail]]** (all fixed live by Zafer Gürel):

- **PUBLISH_DONE forwarded too early with a low stream count** — the relay forwarded PUBLISH_DONE while subgroup streams were still outstanding, so the client's *"delivery semantics not preserved."* moxygen hides a received PUBLISH_DONE until every announced stream has opened; fixed by [#382](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/382) *"hold PUBLISH_DONE until its data streams have arrived."*
- **Track-record race on concurrent PUBLISH** — two PUBLISHes for the same track hit a check-then-insert (`has_track` then `add_track`), so one internal record overwrote the other and a second publisher's objects stopped flowing. Fixed by [#381](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/381) (track-locking in `subscribe_handler`) and [#383](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/383) *"take or create a published track in one step."*
- **DUPLICATE_SUBSCRIPTION after a cancelled subscription** — a SUBSCRIBE re-issued after the prior one was cancelled by stream reset was wrongly rejected.
- **REQUEST_ERROR on the control stream** instead of the request's own bidi stream (draft-18); **no REQUEST_UPDATE handling for PUBLISH** (a FORWARD change was ignored); **FIN instead of reset** when an upstream publisher reset a subgroup mid-stream; **no prefix routing for FETCH** (returned Track Not Found).

- **State transitions**: a nonexistent→existent object-state transition is a **"malformed track" error, not a connection-terminating protocol error**.
- **PR #1770 (default/updatable priority)** was **closed pending fresh mailing-list feedback**; **Top Tracks ([#1830](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1830))** will lean on the newly-merged **`PUBLISH_NOTIFY`** mechanism.
