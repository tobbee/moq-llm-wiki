## WG: post-draft-20 editorial restructuring begins — "Forwarding Preference" becomes "Delivery Mode"

**One day after [[moq-transport]] draft-20 shipped, the editors opened the draft-21 restructuring pass** — the "editorial meeting's output" the [[interim-meetings|interim-2026-moq-21]] minutes named (draft-20 editorial cut → **draft-21 editorial-meeting output** → draft-22 next interop target). All Sep-1, all editorial, no new wire mechanisms:

- **"Draft Restructuring Phase I: move-only edits"** ([#1877](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1877), [[victor-vasiliev|vasilvv]], **+3138/−3110**) — a large section-reordering pass that deliberately moves text without changing it, so the substantive diffs that follow are readable.
- **Rename "Forwarding Preference" → "Delivery Mode"** ([#1886](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1886), [[ian-swett|ianswett]]), with a companion pass removing the old "Forwarding Preference" mentions ([#1891](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1891), vasilvv). A terminology change touching one of MoQT's core concepts (how a track's objects map onto streams/datagrams) — durable enough that implementers and the wiki should start using "Delivery Mode."
- **Structural section moves**: restructure/reorder "Publishing and Receiving Tracks" ([#1885](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1885)/[#1893](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1893), afrind); pull the **Range Filter** ([#1892](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1892)), **Location Filter** ([#1888](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1888)), and **Track Namespace** ([#1887](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1887)) wire formats into their own subsections; add an **Authorization Token Compression** section ([#1882](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1882)); **define the term "subscription"** ([#1884](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1884), vasilvv) and a **Canonical Object Properties** section ([#1883](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1883), ianswett); plus error-code/section moves ([#1878](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1878)–[#1881](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1881)) and the datagram/object-header editorial refactor ([#1874](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1874), suhasHere) that was left OPEN at the draft-20 cut.

## Datatracker & list: the interim-22 (Aug-24) minutes finally post

After sitting unposted for a week — a "minutes pending" line the log carried across ~six updates — the **[[interim-meetings|interim-2026-moq-22]] minutes (Aug-24 virtual interim) landed on the datatracker** (`minutes-interim-2026-moq-22-202608241630`, rev-00, posted 2026-08-31T12:51 UTC) and **[[magnus-westerlund|Magnus Westerlund]] announced them on the mailing list** (Sep-1 08:10 UTC, [permalink](https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/msg/moq/KS4QHlgp4p2bpGzi5kgsr-3q-XM/)). The minutes confirm the Aug-24 session's decisions:

- **Keep the Set-ID-based "or" filters** — the group rejected removing them (useful for property filtering and relay deduplication).
- **Add Parameters to `SUBSCRIBE_NAMESPACE`** (aligning with `PUBLISH_NAMESPACE` extensibility), restricted to **unilateral signaling — no `REQUEST_UPDATE`** (the direction [[ian-swett|Swett]]'s [PR #1858](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1858) took, Fixes [#1800](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/issues/1800)).
- **Cache conflict resolution**: when a relay receives conflicting *immutable* field values, it **ignores the later updates** ([issue #1703](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/issues/1703)).
- **State transitions**: a nonexistent→existent object-state transition is a **"malformed track" error, not a connection-terminating protocol error**.
- **PR #1770 (default/updatable priority)** was **closed pending fresh mailing-list feedback**; **Top Tracks ([#1830](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1830))** will lean on the newly-merged **`PUBLISH_NOTIFY`** mechanism.
- **Action items**: [[mo-zanaty|Mo Zanaty]] + [[ian-swett|Ian Swett]] to write editorial guidance on joining-fetch configuration; Mo to update the Top Tracks PR; editors to take remaining issues to the in-person editorial meeting (Seattle, Oct).

Otherwise the document layer held flat: **draft-ietf-moq-transport-20 stands** (no newer revision), **no new individual draft** was submitted, the **weekly GitHub digest** is not yet due (last Aug-30; next ~Sep-6), and **[[moq-monthly|MoQ Monthly]]** remains at #2. See [[interim-meetings]].
