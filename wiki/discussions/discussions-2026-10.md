## Spec / WG: a subscription flow-control draft, two editorial merges, an auth-token gap

- **New individual draft — [[moq-subscription-flow-control|`draft-frindell-moq-subscription-flow-control-00`]] "Subscription Flow Control Extension for Media over QUIC Transport"** ([[alan-frindell|Frindell]]/Meta + [[ian-swett|Swett]]/Google, posted Oct-7, 12 pp, Standards Track, expires 2027-04-10; [datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-frindell-moq-subscription-flow-control/)) — now the **newest MoQ document**, ahead of Sharma's Oct-6 submission. It adds **subscription-level** flow control (QUIC/WebTransport only bound per-stream and per-session, but one subscription fans out across many subgroup streams): a **SUBSCRIPTION_FLOW_CONTROL** setup option, **MAX_SUB_STREAMS** / **MAX_SUB_BYTES** limits, a **SUB_FLOW_CONTROL_UPDATE** credit-grant message, **SUB_STREAMS_BLOCKED** / **SUB_BYTES_BLOCKED** publisher signals, a per-subscription **Stream Sequence** field in subgroup headers, and **SUBGROUP_RESET** carrying a reset stream's final byte count. The fourth individual MoQ draft in a week (after the two timestamp drafts and Sharma's e2e-timeout). No on-list announcement thread had appeared by this sweep. See [[moq-subscription-flow-control]].

- **New auth-token gap — issue [#1969](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/issues/1969) + PR [#1970](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1970)** (both Oct-7): MOQT defines `EXPIRED_AUTH_TOKEN` as a session-termination, REQUEST_ERROR and stream-reset code, but **not as a PUBLISH_DONE code**, so a publisher ending a live subscription on token expiry can only say `UNAUTHORIZED` or `INTERNAL_ERROR` — a subscriber can't tell "refresh your token and re-subscribe" (routine for long-lived live events) from "you're not allowed here" (don't retry). ## Spec / WG: a new delivery-timeout draft and a collaborative timestamp thread

- **New individual draft — [[moq-end-to-end-delivery-timeout|`draft-sharma-moq-end-to-end-delivery-timeout-00`]] "End-to-End Delivery Timeouts for MOQT"** ([[aman-sharma|Aman Sharma]]/Meta, posted Oct-6, 6 pp, expires 2027-04-09; [datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-sharma-moq-end-to-end-delivery-timeout/)) — now the **newest MoQ document**. It adds an **END_TO_END_DELIVERY_TIMEOUT** setup option and an **END_TO_END_OBJECT_DELIVERY_TIMEOUT** message parameter (varint ms, on SUBSCRIBE / SUBSCRIBE_TRACKS / PUBLISH / REQUEST_UPDATE) so an object carries a **total deadline measured from the original source** instead of a per-hop budget that resets at every relay. Publishers anchor a reference timestamp + local time on the first forwarded object and compute `deadline = reference_time + (T − reference_timestamp)/timescale + timeout`; it's independent of `OBJECT_DELIVERY_TIMEOUT`/`SUBGROUP_DELIVERY_TIMEOUT` with first-to-expire winning. Sharma: *"I just wrote a draft for end-to-end delivery timeouts based on your TIMESTAMP draft and inspired by the discussion Martin started earlier."* See [[moq-end-to-end-delivery-timeout]].
- **The *"Timestamp Draft"* thread turned collaborative (Oct-6)** — replies from [[luke-curley|Luke Curley]], [[aman-sharma|Aman Sharma]], [[cullen-jennings|Cullen Jennings]] and [[steven-riedl|Steven Riedl]] ([thread permalink](https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/msg/moq/kA2TP_PCfKOpKk-jXnaqss875rM/)):
  - **[[alan-frindell|Frindell]]** answered Cullen's "how does this compare to [[moq-tempo|Tempo]]?" by explaining his design **factors out the mechanism for conveying timestamps as properties**, so drafts like Tempo (or Aman's) **assign application semantics on top** rather than compete — Suhas had pointed him to Tempo and he had an agent verify compatibility; he'll add a Tempo reference in -01.
  - **[[luke-curley|Curley]]** was enthusiastic about timestamps generally (a subscriber can ask for *"anything newer than 3s"* instead of juggling timelines; a generic relay can make better caching/delivery decisions), floated that *"maybe timestamps are the way to go"* over group IDs for identity, and noted he **removed DURATION** from his own extension in favour of a timestamped empty frame at each video group's end (to avoid cross-group dependencies and support discontinuities).
  - Net effect: the two timestamp drafts now read as **layerable**, not strictly either/or. See [[moq-timestamp-properties]], [[moq-timestamp]].

## draft-22 is published, and it is not quite wire-identical to -20

[`draft-ietf-moq-transport-22`](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-moq-transport/22/) (165 pages, expires 2027-04-04) is the Seattle interop target named at [[interim-meetings|interim-24]] together with draft-18. Release notes are [PR #1965](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1965) (afrind, approved by [[suhas-nandakumar|Suhas]]).

- **One control-plane change.** `LOCATION_FILTER` (0x21) replaces its Length field with an explicit **Location Filter Type** ([#1953](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1953), [[ian-swett|Swett]], merged Oct-1 18:17 UTC). The types are 0x00 none, 0x01 relative start, 0x02 absolute start, 0x03 + group end, 0x04 absolute range, 0x05 Next Object; any other value is a `PROTOCOL_VIOLATION`. A draft-20/21 decoder will misread it. [[martin-duke|Duke]]'s Sep-21 *"-22 is functionally identical to -20 except the ALPN"* therefore holds everywhere except this one parameter.
- **Everything else is editorial**, most of it merged on `main` from Sep-6 to Sep-11 and held out of the move-only -21. The last piece was [#1946](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1946) (afrind, +243/−190, merged 18:15 UTC after vasilvv's approval four minutes earlier). It restructures Publisher and Namespace Discovery: a Namespace Prefix Matching section, `SUBSCRIBE_TRACKS` moved under Publishing and Receiving Tracks, and a ladder diagram. Per the PR, 2,343 of 2,379 sentences are unchanged.
- **Not in -22**: Zero-length Track Names ([#1954](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1954)) and `DEFAULT PUBLISHER PRIORITY` as a parameter ([#1957](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1957)), both approved, plus `INCLUDE_PAYLOAD` ([#1955](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1955)), the FETCH relative-filter ban ([#1960](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1960)) and Filter Sets ([#1894](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1894)).
