# Activity (Nov 11): three impl repos light up — moqtail 0.18, moq-rs 0.17.1, quiche moqt starts draft-22

A quiet window on the wire and in the WG, but — unusually after a week of moq-dev-only churn — **three tracked implementation repos moved with durable facts on the same day**. No WG document was revised (transport stays **-23**, [[moq-msf|MSF]] stays **-01**, nothing new on the datatracker), and the mailing list produced nothing new since the Nov-9 [[lucas-pardue|Lucas Pardue]] SQUAD-agenda post.

- **[[moqtail]] cut relay@0.18.0 with clustering and a new room-server package.** The Nov-11 release train ([#461](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/461)) tags **relay@0.18.0** and first-publishes a standalone **`room-server@0.1.0`** component, and lands two durable relay capabilities: **clustering** ([#467](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/467)) and **loading relay configuration from a config file** ([#466](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/466)), plus a relay hot-path perf pass ([#462](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/462)) and a TS datagram fix falling back to `createWritable()` when `writable` is missing ([#465](https://github.com/moqtail/moqtail/pull/465)). Clustering + config-file deployment move moqtail toward a deployable multi-node relay.
- **[[moq-rs|cloudflare/moq-rs]] ended its post-Nov-2 quiet with a v0.17.1 observability release.** The release-plz round ([#311](https://github.com/cloudflare/moq-rs/pull/311), Nov-10 21:14) cut the `moq-transport` crate **v0.17.1** — the first tag since the v0.8.0/v0.17.0 draft-18 train — carrying **per-connection usage reporting** ([#310](https://github.com/cloudflare/moq-rs/pull/310), itzmanish). It is an observability feature, not a wire change; the relay still negotiates only `moqt-18`.
- **[[quiche-moq|google/quiche]]'s MoQT dir took its first step onto the draft-22 wire.** Three commits since the Nov-9 one: **`479bc78` *"Change SUBSCRIPTION_FILTER parameter to LOCATION_FILTER and update to MOQT draft-22 format"*** (Nov-10 22:55) — the first draft-22-labelled change in Google's C++ base — plus **`417bcfe`** adding `PUBLISH_STATE_NOTIFY` send/receive (Nov-10 19:08) and a `QUICHE_EXPORT`-annotation fix (Nov-11).
