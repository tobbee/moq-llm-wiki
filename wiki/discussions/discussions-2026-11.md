# Activity (Nov 5): MPEG Systems replies on LOCMAF; another quiet nightly

The one material development in an otherwise quiet post-deadline window: **ISO/IEC JTC1/SC29/WG3 (MPEG Systems) sent its reply liaison on [[moq-locmaf|LOCMAF]]** — the response the LOCMAF adoption call has been gated on since Sep-28. No WG document was revised (transport stays **-23**, [[moq-msf|MSF]] stays **-01**), no new draft hit the datatracker, and the interop nightly drifted −2.

- **MPEG Systems' LOCMAF reply liaison is on the list (the awaited gate response).** On **Nov-4 14:02 UTC**, **Stephan Wenger** (the IETF's liaison officer toward MPEG) posted *"[Reply liaison from MPEG Systems]"* to `moq@ietf.org` ([message](https://mailarchive.ietf.org/arch/msg/moq/uJYkXAr3QkI_xPw9TUgsq9llTUc/)), attaching MPEG Systems output document **N1956, *"Liaison to IETF MOQ WG on LOCMAF"*** (approved at the Oct-2026 Hangzhou meeting, dated **2026-10-23**, Expected action *None*). This answers the WG's outgoing [liaison 2285](https://datatracker.ietf.org/liaison/2285/) (sent Sep-28, reply requested by Oct-30), which [[magnus-westerlund|Westerlund]] had tied the formal adoption call to. The substance: MPEG **welcomes** LOCMAF; reports it has **started studying low-overhead CMAF profiles / reusable media-format definitions** (and already built a low-overhead *image* format for an analogous small-asset need); **invites IETF LOCMAF contributors into a joint MPEG Ad hoc Group** (publicly accessible to non-MPEG members); floats **defining the low-overhead-CMAF work inside an MPEG core specification** that MOQ and others could reference; raises a **signaling concern** (an IETF-published LOCMAF means LOCMAF bytes used in endpoints beyond MOQ, where CMAF bytes are expected, "without proper signaling"); and **requests the WG take these considerations into account before opening a formal adoption call** and inform MPEG of its plans, offering to have MPEG experts join a MOQ meeting. Wenger notes the formal datatracker copy will likely not appear before IETF 127 (San Francisco). It is **not an objection** — but it turns the open question from *"may we standardize LOCMAF?"* into *"IETF draft vs. MPEG spec vs. jointly-maintained artifact?"*, a decision now for the WG, plausibly at IETF 127. No WG members had replied on-list as of this writing. See [[moq-locmaf]].
