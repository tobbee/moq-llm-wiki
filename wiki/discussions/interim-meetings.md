> **2026-06-23 note**: **The June 22 interim is now on the record as `interim-2026-moq-17` — minutes are posted and they resolve the SSTS/switching design, which had been the WG's biggest open question all spring.** The off-calendar meeting the wiki flagged June 22 *did* get a datatracker entry retroactively (as **[interim-2026-moq-17](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/minutes-interim-2026-moq-17-202606221630/)**, **not** the `-12` the wiki had guessed from the old numbering), and [[martin-duke|Martin Duke]] posted **"Yesterday's minutes available"** to the list June 23. Substance of the 16:30-UTC session:
> - **DTS is renamed to SSTS — "Sender Side Track Switching"** (*"this title more accurately reflects the functionality,"* RFC editors retain final naming authority). This retires the "DTS" label the wiki has tracked since the May SWITCH/DTS consensus call.
> - **[[will-law|Will Law]] presented a pre-recorded [[yu-you|Yu You]]/Nokia demo**: three tracks (500/1500/3000 kbps) in one switching set, a relay receiving all three but forwarding only one, *"smooth, continuous switching by toggling the forward state,"* stable 1080p when switching is disabled, and successful handling of *"fast-frequency switching close to segment boundaries"* under aggressive throughput oscillation.
> - **Core consensus**: (1) **extensible switching algorithm** keyed by a **numeric ID in an IANA table**, negotiated via an **array of preferred algorithms** (client requests, relay replies with supported set); (2) the current implementation becomes the mandatory **"Algorithm Zero"** baseline in the base spec, future algorithms register + negotiate; (3) **remove the DDoS-protection negotiation properties** (the per-switching-set concurrent-track / throughput limits Gwendal raised on DTS [PR #1638](https://github.com/moq-wg/moq-transport/pull/1638)) — rely instead on **authorization tokens + existing relay-side protections**; (4) **keep single-message switching-set assignment** (the group decided *against* splitting it into multiple messages); (5) **unsubscription auto-removes a track from the switching set** (no explicit removal message).
> - **Draft-19 phased review**: [[alan-frindell|Alan Frindell]] proposed prioritizing ~**50% of the ~18 open PRs for immediate review**, the remainder *"for the period just before the deadline"*; Duke supported the sequencing. The merge bar for **"Design" PRs** is **two weeks open + stamps from the four editors/authors** — which is exactly why only 2 PRs merged June 22 and the rest of the backlog stays open *by design*, not as a stall. Design PRs targeting draft-19 had to be **published June 22** to clear the two-week window before the **July 6** deadline.
> - **Admin**: the **Authorization Design Team** enrollment closes **June 26**; the October hybrid-interim **venue poll** closes **July 5**; the **next interim is July 6** (= **[interim-2026-moq-18](https://datatracker.ietf.org/meeting/interim-2026-moq-18/)**, IESG-announced June 22, focused on resolving open `draft-ietf-moq-transport` issues); MOQ has **3 slots at IETF 126 / Vienna**, with the late-Friday slot reserved for the non-transport drafts (MSF, LOC, C4M, privacy-pass-auth, CMSF, secure-objects). See [[switch-abr]], [[moq-transport]], [[discussions-2026-06]].
